Latent Representation Learning

Given:   \(\mathcal{D} = \{(X^{(i)}, y^{(i)})\}_{i=1}^{N}\)   where   \(X \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times D}\),   \(y \in \mathbb{R}\)
Encoder
\(q_{\phi}(Z \mid X)\)
Latent \(Z\)
\(\mathcal{N}(0, I)\)
Decoder
\(p_{\theta}(X \mid Z)\)
\[ \mathcal{L}_{\text{ELBO}}(X) = \underbrace{ \mathbb{E}_{q_{\phi}(Z \mid X)} \big[\log p_{\theta}(X \mid Z)\big] }_{\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}} \text{: Reconstruction}} - \underbrace{ D_{\text{KL}} \big( q_{\phi}(Z \mid X) \parallel p(Z) \big) }_{\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}} \text{: KL Regularization}} \]
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}}\)
• Keeps meaningful information in latent \(Z\)
• Enables accurate reconstruction
\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}\)
• Penalizes memorizing input-specific details
• Encourages generalizable latent structure

Mutual Information

Rewrite the regularization term over the full dataset
Decompose into Mutual Information + Posterior Matching
\[ \mathbb{E}_{p(X)}[\mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}(X)] = \underbrace{ \mathbb{E}_{q_{\phi}(Z, X)} \left[ \log \frac{q_{\phi}(Z \mid X)} {q_{\phi}(Z)} \right] }_{{\text{I}_{q_{\phi}}(X;Z)}} + \underbrace{ D_{\text{KL}} \big(q_{\phi}(Z)\parallel p(Z)\big) }_{\text{posterior matching}} \]
InfoVAE-Med3D loss: reweight mutual information and posterior matching
\[ \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoVAE-Med3D}} = \mathbb{E}_{p(X)} \big[\mathcal{L}_{\text{rec}}(X)\big] - \alpha\,\text{I}_{q_{\phi}}(X;Z) - \beta\, D_{\text{KL}} \big(q_{\phi}(Z)\parallel p(Z)\big) \]
Mutual information: retained information from \(X\) in latent \(Z\)
Posterior matching: aligns aggregate posterior \(q_{\phi}(Z)\) with prior \(p(Z)\)

Downstream Prediction

Extract latent representations:
\[ z = \mathcal{E}(X) \in \mathbb{R}^{d}, \quad d \ll H \times W \times D \]
Prediction: SVR maps latent representations to biomarker outcomes (brain age, SDMT score):
\[ f(z) = \langle w, z \rangle + b \approx y \]
💡 Key Insight
The model predicts CI-related biomarkers from meaningful information encoded in the latent representations.

Latent Space Analysis

Project latent representations \(Z \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d}\) by \(W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times k}\).
Choose \(k = 2\) to project into 2D space:
\[ Z_2 = ZW \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 2} \]
PCA
Unsupervised · Variance analysis
\[ \max_{W}\ \mathrm{Tr}(W^\top \Sigma W) \]
Captures dominant variance directions in the latent space
PLSRgression
Supervised · Target-aware analysis
\[ \max_{W}\ \mathrm{Cov}(Z_cW, Y_c) \]
Preserves task-relevant latent structures for CI-related biomarkers prediction

04
Conclusion

Main Findings

RQ1
InfoVAE-Med3D captures more brain structural information, outperforming baseline reconstruction.
RQ2
Latent space encodes meaningful information (gender, GM/WM volume).
RQ3
Effective CI-related biomarker prediction (SDMT, brain age) with interpretability.
✅ Hypothesis Validated
Latent representation learning successfully encodes meaningful structural and demographic information from 3D brain MRI, enabling effective and interpretable CI-related biomarker predictions.

Limitations & Future Work

⚠️ Limitations
VAE-inherent: blurred reconstructions, limiting visual image-latent interpretation.
Limited scope: focused on CI biomarker predictions, without disease severity and progression prediction.
Single modality: only T1-weighted MRI is used, while other modalities (e.g., T2, FLAIR) may provide complementary information.
🌱 Future Work
Advanced GenAI: Explore stronger generative models for improved latent representations with controllable latent manipulation.
Multi-modal MRI: Incorporate additional MRI modalities (T2, FLAIR) for richer and more robust representations.
