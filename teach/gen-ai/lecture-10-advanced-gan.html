1.
Wasserstein GANs

The Diagnosis

  • JS divergence saturates when supports barely overlap
  • Two image distributions on low-dimensional manifolds rarely overlap
  • A perfect discriminator gives the generator zero gradient

Wasserstein Distance

$$ W(p, q) = \inf_{\gamma \in \Pi(p,q)} \mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim\gamma}\big[\|x - y\|\big] $$
  • The earth mover's distance: least cost to move one pile onto the other
  • Finite and informative even with disjoint supports
  • Its gradient keeps pointing the right way

The Kantorovich Duality

$$ W(p,q) = \sup_{\|f\|_L \le 1} \quad \mathbb{E}_{p}[f(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{q}[f(x)] $$
  • The infimum becomes a supremum over 1-Lipschitz functions
  • The critic \( f \) replaces the discriminator
  • It outputs a real score, not a probability

WGAN and WGAN-GP

  • WGAN: enforce Lipschitzness by clipping weights — crude
  • WGAN-GP: penalise the gradient norm instead
  • $$ \lambda \, \mathbb{E}\big[(\|\nabla_{\hat{x}} D(\hat{x})\|_2 - 1)^2\big] $$
  • The critic loss now correlates with sample quality

2.
Conditional and Style-Based GANs

Conditional GANs

$$ \min_G \max_D \quad \mathbb{E}[\log D(x, c)] + \mathbb{E}[\log(1 - D(G(z, c), c))] $$
  • Feed the condition \( c \) to both networks
  • The discriminator judges whether the pair is consistent
  • The route to class-conditional and text-conditional generation

Progressive Growing

  • Start at \( 4\times4 \) and add layers as training proceeds
  • Each resolution stabilises before the next is introduced
  • The first method to reach megapixel faces

StyleGAN

  • Map \( z \) into an intermediate space \( \mathcal{W} \) before use
  • Inject style at every resolution via AdaIN
  • Add per-layer noise for stochastic detail

Why the Style Space Matters

  • \( \mathcal{W} \) is less entangled than the Gaussian prior
  • Coarse layers control pose, fine layers control texture
  • This is what made semantic image editing practical

3.
Image-to-Image Translation

Image-to-Image Translation

Learn a mapping between two image domains rather than from noise.
  • Sketch → photo, day → night, segmentation map → scene
  • The condition is now an entire image
  • A conditional GAN where \( c \) is spatially structured

pix2pix

  • Requires paired training data
  • U-Net generator preserves spatial detail via skip connections
  • PatchGAN discriminator judges local patches, not whole images
  • Adversarial loss combined with an \( \ell_1 \) reconstruction term

The Pairing Problem

  • Aligned pairs are expensive or impossible to collect
  • There is no photo of the same scene painted by Monet
  • We need translation from unpaired collections

CycleGAN

$$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{cyc}} = \mathbb{E}\big[\|F(G(x)) - x\|_1\big] + \mathbb{E}\big[\|G(F(y)) - y\|_1\big] $$
  • Two generators \( G: X \to Y \) and \( F: Y \to X \)
  • Cycle consistency: translating there and back must return the original
  • This constraint replaces the missing pairs

4.
Stabilisation and Legacy

Stabilisation Techniques

  • Spectral normalisation — constrain the discriminator's Lipschitz constant
  • Two time-scale update rule — different learning rates for \( G \) and \( D \)
  • Self-attention — capture long-range structure

Fighting Mode Collapse

  • Minibatch discrimination: let \( D \) see batch-level diversity
  • Unrolled GANs: let \( G \) anticipate \( D \)'s response
  • Packing: feed several samples to \( D \) at once

Where GANs Stand Today

  • Still the fastest: one forward pass per sample
  • Still excellent within a narrow domain such as faces
  • Diffusion overtook them on diversity and on text-to-image
  • The adversarial loss survives inside VQGAN and in distillation

Summary

  • Wasserstein distance fixes the saturating-gradient problem
  • WGAN-GP enforces the Lipschitz constraint with a gradient penalty
  • Conditional GANs feed the condition to both networks
  • StyleGAN introduces a disentangled style space
  • CycleGAN uses cycle consistency to avoid needing paired data
